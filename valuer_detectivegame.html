The Valuer

You've dealt as a broker and steered assets as a manager. Now the hardest question: what's it actually worth? Your time is short and not every source is worth chasing — spend it where it counts.

Two methods, and a nose for evidence
Comparables for homes; the investment method (value the income) for let property. But the real test is your time: you can't investigate everything, and sources vary — some are biased, some are noise, and a few genuinely predict where the market's heading. Miss the decisive clue and your figure will be wrong.
How to play

The Method

⏳
1. Time is tight — choose wisely
You get only 5 units of time, but there are far more things you could investigate, and some cost 2. You physically can't do it all. Deciding what's worth your time is the first skill.
🕵️
2. Not all evidence is equal
Some sources are biased (the seller wants a high figure). Some are noise that tells you little. And a few are predictive — a planning approval, an employer arriving — that reveal where the market's going. You won't know which until you spend the time.
⚠️
3. Miss the key clue, be wrong
A decisive clue can change what the property is truly worth. Skip it and you'll value the wrong thing — the debrief will show you what you missed and what it was worth.
⚖️
4. Then value it — the right way
Adjust comparables (homes), or run the income method (let property — the game does the maths, you make the judgements). Commit a figure you can defend.
