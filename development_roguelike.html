Consent

A derelict site, a scheme to fight for, and someone across the table fighting back. Build a deck of tactics, read your opponent, and survive the run from acquisition to decision. The right card depends on the moment — and your rival hits back every turn.

It's a duel, not a puzzle
Each stage is a negotiation against a live opponent who attacks your key metric every turn — and you'll see it coming, so defend or race. Cards aren't just big numbers: the right tactic lands hard in the right situation and flops in the wrong one. Time your trump cards, chain combos, and don't let your lifeline hit zero.

The Developer

Lifeline: Viability. Your rival is the Planning Authority — it demands affordable housing, threatens refusal, rallies objectors. Wear down each obstacle before it grinds your numbers to nothing.

The Planner
Extract public value — and still deliver
Lifeline: Public value. Your rival is the Developer — viability reports, lobbying your chair, threats to walk. Hold the public interest without strangling the scheme.

How a run works
  • 🗺️The process map: move through the real stages of development — . Each is a fight; a draft follows each win.
  • ⚔️The opponent telegraphs: at the top of your turn you see its next move (e.g. "−9 to your lifeline"). Shield it, or clear the obstacle first. It also reacts to some cards you play.
  • 🎯Context matters: a card can be strong here and weak there — no hints. Learn the matchups. Each obstacle has a soft spot to find.
  • ✨Combos & trumps: some card pairs chain for a bonus. Your most powerful cards work once per fight — time them.
  • 💀Permadeath: lifeline to zero and the scheme collapses. Damage carries between fights — survive to the Decision.
