A derelict site and a scheme worth fighting for. Build a deck of tactics, read each challenge as it comes, and get the scheme from acquisition to decision in one piece. The right card depends on the moment — and every challenge pushes back while you work.

It's a duel, not a puzzle
Read the moment, play the right lever
Every stage is a real development challenge that pushes back on your key metric each turn — and you'll see it coming, so shield it or clear the challenge first. Cards fall into four kinds of lever: negotiation, financial, design and resilience. Each one shines in some situations and flounders in others. Learn which lever fits which challenge, time your trump cards, chain a combo or two, and keep your lifeline off the floor.
🏗️
The Developer
Push the scheme through, profitably
Lifeline: Viability. You see every challenge through a developer's eyes — affordable housing asks, rising costs, a nervous lender, the committee vote. Keep the numbers standing while you wear each one down.
🏛️
The Planner
Extract public value — and still deliver
Lifeline: Public value. You see the same challenges through the council's eyes — weak schemes, heritage at risk, wavering councillors. Hold the public interest while keeping the scheme deliverable enough to actually happen.

How a run works
  • 🗺️The process map: move through the real stages of development — . Each is a fight; a draft follows each win.
  • ⚔️The challenge builds: at the top of each turn you see what's coming (say, "−12 to your lifeline"). Shield it, or clear the challenge before it lands. A few challenges also react to specific cards you play.
  • 🎯Context matters: a card can be strong here and weak there — no hints. Learn the matchups. Each obstacle has a soft spot to find.
  • ✨Combos & trumps: some card pairs chain for a bonus. Your most powerful cards work once per fight — time them.
  • 💀Permadeath: lifeline to zero and the scheme collapses. Damage carries between fights — survive to the Decision.
