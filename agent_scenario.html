How it works
Choices move three meters — client Trust, your Commission, and Reputation — plus your Energy (⚡ only 4 pips). Diligent work and hard conversations cost energy; cutting corners is cheap but bites you later. You can't do everything well — pick your battles, because if energy hits zero you burn out and quit mid-job. The teaching notes on each decision are saved for your debrief at the end.

The lesson
Diligence is essential but finite. A good agent can't do everything to a gold standard — the skill is triage: spend energy on the high-stakes moments (disclosure, contracts, real negotiation) and travel lighter on the rest. Burnout is a genuine occupational risk in a commission-driven, client-facing job.
