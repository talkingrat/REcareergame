Career Scenario · Property & Asset Management

The Assistant
Asset Manager

It's your first job in property. You're the assistant to an asset manager — the person who looks after a building for the investor who owns it. You won't run the show yet; your job is to look into things, spot problems, and bring your manager good options. Pick where you'll start.

🏘️
Steady income · Easier start
A Rented Flats Building
A block of flats that people rent to live in. The owner just wants a calm, reliable income and happy tenants who stay. Your job: help keep it that way. Great place to learn the ropes.
Difficulty: ★☆☆ · about 5 short decisions
🛍️
Fixing it up · A step up
A Tired Shopping Centre
A run-down shopping centre the owner bought cheap, hoping to do it up and sell for more. Your job: help spend the money wisely, fix which shops are in it, and prove it's worth more. Trickier.
Difficulty: ★★☆ · about 5 short decisions
New to all this? Good.
You don't need to know any property jargon. Each building starts with a quick "who's who," your manager explains anything technical as it comes up, and every meter has a plain-English note if you tap it. You'll pick up the real terms (like NOI or void) by seeing what they mean in the story.
